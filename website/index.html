Frequently Asked Questions

What is a pre-purchase car inspection and why do I need one?

A pre-purchase car inspection is an independent, 160+ point examination of a used car's engine, transmission, suspension, brakes, electricals, structure, interior and paperwork carried out before you buy. It reveals hidden accidental repairs, odometer tampering, flood damage and pending repairs so you can negotiate the right price or walk away. InspectionWale delivers a same-day digital report with photos.

How much does a car inspection cost at InspectionWale?

A standard pre-purchase inspection starts at ₹1,399. The fee covers a certified inspector visiting the car's location, the full 160+ point checklist, a test drive where possible, and a detailed report delivered the same day.

Which areas do you serve?

We inspect cars across the Mumbai Metropolitan Region including Mumbai, Navi Mumbai, Thane, Kalyan, Dombivli, Badlapur, Kandivali, Borivali, Andheri, Bandra, Mira-Bhayandar, Vasai-Virar, Bhiwandi, Ambernath, Ulhasnagar, Panvel and Pune.

How long does an inspection take and when do I get the report?

A typical inspection takes 60–90 minutes at the car's location. Your detailed report with photographs and ratings is delivered the same day, usually within a few hours of the inspection.

Can I sell or list my car on InspectionWale?

Yes. Use the "List / Sell Your Car" form to submit your car details and photos. Once reviewed and approved, your car appears in our verified used-car marketplace where buyers can view it and book an inspection.

Do you inspect the car at my home or the seller's location?

Yes. Our inspectors travel to wherever the car is parked — the seller's home, a dealership or a parking lot — anywhere within our service cities, at your chosen date and time slot.

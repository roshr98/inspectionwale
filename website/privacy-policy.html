Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 August 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how InspectionWale (“InspectionWale”, “we”, “us” or “our”) collects, uses and protects information when you use inspectionwale.com, request a vehicle inspection, submit a marketplace listing, contact us or otherwise use our services.

Depending on how you use the website, we may collect information you provide, such as your name, telephone number, email address, city, vehicle and booking details, listing information, photographs, messages and other information submitted through our forms. We use information to respond to enquiries, arrange and deliver inspection services, create and manage marketplace listings, communicate about bookings, improve website performance and security, prevent misuse, maintain business records, comply with legal obligations and measure the effectiveness of our services and advertising.

We do not sell your personal information. Service providers are expected to use information only for the services they provide to us.

Data retention and security

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, including providing services, resolving disputes, maintaining records and meeting legal obligations. We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards, but no internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure.

Your choices and rights

You may ask us to access, correct or delete personal information associated with you, subject to applicable law and legitimate record-keeping requirements. You may also control cookies through your browser and advertising preferences through the links above.
